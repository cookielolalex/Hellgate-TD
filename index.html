How this deck wins

怎麼用這副牌贏瑞士輪

Kai'Sa is not a linear aggro deck and it is not a tap-out control deck. It is a spell-tempo deck: cheap Action and Reaction spells keep battlefields empty or skinny, small units convert those empty lanes into points, and then Darius, Watcher, or Time Warp ends the game before the opponent rebuilds.

Score to 8. You Hold during your beginning phase if you already control a battlefield, and you Conquer when you take one you have not scored this turn. The final point has extra rules: a Hold can win, but a Conquer for the last point only counts if you have scored every battlefield this turn. If you Conquer for point 8 without having scored the other field, you draw a card instead of winning. Learn that rule cold before Taiwan. Time Warp exists to turn a 6- or 7-point board into a Hold win they never get to answer.

Legend first

Exhaust Kai'Sa, Daughter of the Void whenever you would otherwise recycle a rune to pay a spell's Power. That is almost every Hextech Ray, Falling Star, and Icathian Rain. If you forget the legend, you deramp and the deck feels clunky. If you remember it, you cast a spell most turns and still channel runes for tomorrow.

Turn shape

  1. Opener. Keep Ravenbloom Student, Hextech Ray, Lecturing Yordle, or Retreat. A 2-drop plus a 1-cost spell is the real keep. Ship Time Warp back unless you already know they are a ramp deck.
  2. Lanes, not hero-trading. Put a small unit on an empty battlefield and score. Do not pick a 4-vs-4 fight without a spell in reserve.
  3. Survivor loop. Play Kai'Sa, Survivor Accelerated when a lane is winnable. Conquer, draw. If they threaten the Hold, Retreat her, channel a rune, recast later. One Survivor doing this twice is more points than slamming Darius into a ready blocker.
  4. Midgame Legion. Spell, then Noxus Hopeful for 2. Or spell, then Darius to ready a 7-might attacker. Always count "is this my second card?" before you play the 5-drop.
  5. Close. Accelerated Watcher shrinks their board and takes a field. Second attacker takes the other. Or Warp at 6 holding two, or at 7 holding one. Icathian Rain is for the turn their board is actually wider than your pings.

Energy discipline

Leave Energy up on their turn. This list is full of Action and Reaction cards that only matter in showdowns. Tapping out to deploy a 4-might unit into an open lane is often correct; tapping out when they have a ready attacker on your field is how Kai'Sa loses to Master Yi and Annie.

Mulligan

  • Always: Student, Ray, Yordle, Retreat, a rune-friendly 2-drop.
  • On the play vs unknown: keep one interaction and one unit. Do not keep Watcher + Warp + Rain.
  • On the draw vs known aggro: Ray, Stupefy, Smoke Screen, Student.
  • Vs known slow decks: you may keep Warp if the rest of the hand can live until 10 Energy.

Five lines to memorize

五條固定連招 · 能量算清楚再動手

1. Legion Hopeful

  1. 1. Hextech Ray or Cleave
  2. 2. Exhaust legend for the spell Power
  3. 3. Noxus Hopeful for 2 Energy, 4 Might

If you play Hopeful first, you pay 4 and waste Legion. Same window as Darius: the cheap card is always first.

2. Ready Darius

  1. 1. 1-cost spell (Ray / Cleave / Stupefy)
  2. 2. Darius — trigger +2 Might and Ready
  3. 3. Conquer at 7

Darius played as the first card of the turn sits exhausted at 5. That is a brick. Count “second card” out loud.

3. Survivor loop

  1. 1. Accelerate Survivor (4E + 1E + 1 Fury Power)
  2. 2. Conquer, draw
  3. 3. If they win the swing-back: Retreat, channel 1 exhausted rune, recast later

Do not Accelerate into a ready bigger body with no spell backup. An empty lane is the green light.

4. Watcher two-for

  1. 1. Play Watcher, pay Accelerate (7E + 1E + 1 Mind Power)
  2. 2. Enemy units −3 Might (min 1)
  3. 3. Conquer field A, send a second attacker at field B

The shrink lasts the rest of the turn. This is how you take two fields in one go without Time Warp.

5. Time Warp wins

  1. A. You are at 6 and Hold two fields → Warp → both Holds for 8
  2. B. You are at 7, Hold one field → Warp → that Hold is 8
  3. C. You Conquer to 7, cannot legally take 8 from the other field → Warp into the Hold

10 Energy + 4 Mind Power, then this card banishes. Ask if they have a deny (Wind Wall / Calm) before you tap the runes. If they do, you just spent your turn and your closer.

Scoring traps

7→8
You Conquer the last empty field but did not score the other one this turn. That is not a win. You draw a card. Hold next turn, or score both fields this turn, or Warp.
6
Holding two fields at the start of your turn is already two points (8). You do not need to attack. If you are at 6 and only Hold one, you still need another score.
Rain
Icathian Rain is six separate 2-damage hits (errata). Stack 12 on a fatty or spray a wide board. Do not fire it into a single 3-might unit Hextech Ray already kills.

Origins matchups to respect

起源環境要小心的對局

Taiwan's first month is Origins-legal product. The same legends that ran western and CN Origins events will show up at store-level Summoner Skirmish and at the September Regional Qualifier. Kai'Sa is a premier Origins legend. It still has losing and coin-flip pairings. Play those, do not ignore them.

Legend繁中RolePlan
Master Yi 易大師 Worst regular pairing. Wuju tempo-defense out-sizes cheap pings and contests both lanes. Do not empty both fields. Smoke Screen and Falling Star are for his mid-might attackers, not for their 2-drops. Watcher shrink is the clean swing. If they sit behind Tank units, Cleave a second attacker instead of Ray-ing into Tank. Never Time Warp into a board they will Hold-kill; Warp only when a Hold wins on the extra turn.
Viktor 維克特 Gear grind. Recycles value, clogs lanes with bodies your 3-damage spells do not finish. Kill the gear plan early if you own Thermo Beam in the board. Otherwise punch points now. Do not play a long game of 1-for-1s. Hopeful and Accelerated Survivor force him off fields. Rain is actually good here if he swarms small units. Side in extra Falling Star / Smoke if you have them.
Annie 安妮 Explosive early. Can race you before Watcher comes online. Keep Ray and Stupefy. Block her first conquer. Do not greed Dreaming Tree draws on turn two. A clunky opener vs Annie is a lost match. After you stabilize, you are favored — she runs out of gas, you do not.
Kai'Sa mirror 卡莎鏡像 Same tools. Sequencing and Survivor timing decide it. Void Gate is the most important battlefield. Do not be the first to tap out of Reactions. Winner is whoever gets a ready Survivor conquer plus a card, then Retreats out of the swing-back. Hold Smoke Screen for their Darius or Watcher, not their Sentry.
Darius / Sett 達瑞斯 / 賽特 Fat midrange. Sett walls; enemy Darius uses your own Legion trick. Falling Star stacked (6) or Smoke Screen −4. Do not Cleave into a bigger ready body. Watcher is excellent. You are generally favored if you do not donate free conquers on turn two.
Jinx, Lee Sin, Ahri, Volibear 吉茵珂絲、李星、阿璃、弗力貝爾 Origins data had them below the S-tier pair. Still capable of stealing games. Play the main plan. Vs Jinx, respect wide boards and save Rain. Vs Lee Sin, do not walk Survivor into a ready monk without a Reaction. Vs Ahri, they can score without a clean Hold — race. Vs Volibear, Warp is live if they spend the first four turns ramping.
Miss Fortune / Teemo 好運姐 / 提摩 Trick and tempo. Less common, high variance. Do not overcommit into hidden combat tricks. Keep one Reaction. Take points when lanes are truly empty.
Store-level warning. First-month Taiwan events will still have a lot of precon Jinx / Viktor / Lee Sin and Proving Grounds Annie / Lux / Yi / Garen. Those are winnable if you play real constructed. The players who will knock you out of a Regional Qualifier are Yi, Viktor, Annie, the mirror, and anyone who knows the final-point rule better than you.

Learn this before you sit down

賽前必練 · 規則比記錯卡還容易把你淘汰

Rules that decide matches

  1. Hold vs Conquer, and the final point exception.
  2. Action (your turn or showdowns) vs Reaction (almost any time, including before things resolve).
  3. Accelerate is +1 Energy and +1 matching Power, optional, paid as you play the unit.
  4. Power is recycling a rune, not exhausting it. Exhausting pays Energy. Kai'Sa's legend pays one spell Power without recycling.
  5. Legion, Deathknell, Tank, Deflect, Assault — say them out loud in English and Chinese.
  6. Showdown math: might vs might, leftover units, who is ready afterward.
  7. You may only score each battlefield once per turn.
  8. Marked cards, notes, and slow play are how otherwise good players get warnings.

Practice plan (do this, then register)

  1. Goldfish ten opening hands. Write keep/mull.
  2. Play the Darius line (1-cost then 5-drop) until it is automatic.
  3. Play the Watcher Accelerate line and name which two fields you are taking.
  4. Set a board at 6 and at 7 points and practice the Warp win plus the failed-Warp (deny) branch.
  5. Play at least one real Skirmish or shop night. Physical sequencing is slower than you think.
  6. Read the current Tournament Rules and the Falling Star / Icathian Rain errata. Judges will use the new text.
