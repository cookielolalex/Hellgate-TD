Legend: Kai'Sa, Daughter of the Void 卡莎，虛空之女 · Runes 7 Fury / 5 Mind · Fields: Void Gate 虛空之門, Dreaming Tree 幻夢之樹, Zaun Warrens 祖安地道. 1 Survivor starts Chosen.

Do this every game

  1. Exhaust legend before recycling a rune for a spell.
  2. Spell first, then Hopeful (2E) or Darius (ready 7).
  3. Accelerate Survivor only if the lane is winnable. Conquer = draw.
  4. Leave Energy up on their turn (Ray / Stupefy / Retreat / Smoke).
  5. Close with Accelerated Watcher (−3 all) or Warp at 6 holding two / 7 holding one.

Scoring (first to 8)

  • Hold = you already control it at your beginning phase.
  • Conquer = you take a field you have not scored this turn.
  • Each field once per turn.
  • Last point: Hold can win. Conquer for 8 only if you scored every field this turn. Otherwise you draw a card and do not win.

Mulligan

  • Keep: Student + Ray / Yordle / Retreat.
  • Ship: Watcher + Warp + Rain together.
  • Vs Annie/Yi: keep interaction.

Matchups

  • 易大師 Yi — worst. Do not empty both lanes. Smoke/Star/Watcher. Warp only if Hold wins.
  • 維克特 Viktor — do not 1-for-1 forever. Points now. Rain if he goes wide.
  • 安妮 Annie — keep Ray/Stupefy. Stabilize, then you win the long game.
  • 卡莎 mirror — Void Gate. Do not tap out of Reactions. Smoke their Darius/Watcher.
  • 吉茵珂絲 / 李星 — main plan. Save Rain vs Jinx wide.
